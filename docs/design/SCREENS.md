# Screens

## Primary navigation

1. **Home:** storage overview, last scan, permission coverage, and Scan Mac.
2. **Cleanup:** Ready to clear, Needs your review, Protected, plus Duplicate and Developer/AI workflows.
3. **Explore:** folder breadcrumb, treemap/list switch, size and age filters.
4. **Applications:** installed apps, total footprint, and evidence-backed related files.
5. **History:** operation outcomes, pending Trash, eligible restore, and failures.

## Supporting surfaces

- Welcome and first scan.
- Contextual permission explanation.
- Finding detail/evidence panel.
- Review plan and destructive-action sheet.
- Honest completion summary.
- Settings, diagnostics export, and About.

Each screen must cover empty, loading, partial, permission-limited, cancelled, error, and success states where applicable.
