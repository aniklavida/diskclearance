# Responsive behaviour

DiskClearance is a desktop application, but its window must remain usable from 760 × 560 px upward.

- At compact width, the sidebar collapses to labelled icons and secondary inspectors become sheets.
- Tables preserve name, size, class, and selection; lower-priority columns move into detail disclosure.
- Treemap and list are switchable rather than squeezed side by side.
- Review totals and the primary action remain visible without covering content.
- Large windows limit text line length and use space for evidence or comparison, not oversized empty cards.
- Light/dark mode and 200% zoom are verified at minimum and common window sizes.
