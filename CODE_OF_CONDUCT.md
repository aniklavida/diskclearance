# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking the impact of words and actions seriously.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus discussion on the work.
- Protect private information and prioritize user safety.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, or retaliation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or ban contributions and participants when necessary to protect the community.
